About

NorthLedger Tools makes calculators and spreadsheets that show their work. Each free calculator here uses the same formulas as the matching NorthLedger spreadsheet, which adds year-round logging and tracking. You can find them on our Gumroad store. Rates are updated when governments publish new figures, normally each January.

These tools give estimates, not tax or financial advice. The Canada Revenue Agency and Revenu Québec are the official sources for your own situation.
