How this 2026 calculation works

It mirrors the steps of the IRS's own Form 1040-ES worksheets: (1) self-employment earnings = net profit × 92.35%; (2) Social Security 12.4% on earnings up to the $184,500 wage base minus your W-2 wages, plus Medicare 2.9%; (3) half of that tax is deducted from income; (4) standard deduction ($16,100 single, $32,200 joint, $24,150 head of household); (5) the QBI deduction; (6) tax from the 2026 brackets (10% to 37%); (7) the Additional Medicare Tax if your earnings are high; (8) required annual payment = the smaller of 90% of the 2026 total or 100% (110%) of the 2025 tax, minus withholding, split in four.

Frequently asked questions

How much is self-employment tax in 2026?

15.3% of 92.35% of your net profit: 12.4% Social Security on the first $184,500 of combined wages and self-employment earnings, plus 2.9% Medicare on everything (no cap). So $100,000 of profit has $92,350 of earnings and $14,129.55 of self-employment tax. If you owe less than $400 of net earnings, you do not owe it.

Do I pay self-employment tax on 1099 income if I also have a W-2 job?

Yes, on the 1099 profit. But Social Security tax is capped at $184,500 of wages plus self-employment earnings combined, so W-2 wages use up part of the cap first; this calculator subtracts your W-2 Social Security wages before applying the 12.4%. Medicare tax is not capped.

What is the Additional Medicare Tax and does it apply to me?

An extra 0.9% on Medicare wages and self-employment earnings above $200,000 (single or head of household) or $250,000 (married filing jointly), reported on Form 8959. It is included in the total above. This tool uses only your own wages, so a married couple whose combined wages are high should add the Form 8959 worksheet result.

How is the quarterly estimated tax payment worked out?

Form 1040-ES uses the smaller of 90% of your expected 2026 total tax (income tax plus self-employment tax) or 100% of your 2025 tax (110% if your 2025 AGI was over $150,000). Subtract income tax withheld from any job, and divide by four. You do not have to pay if you expect to owe under $1,000 after withholding.

What are the 2026 estimated tax due dates?

April 15, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027. You can skip the January 15, 2027 payment if you file your 2026 return by February 1, 2027 and pay the full balance with it.

What is the QBI deduction and how big is it here?

The qualified business income deduction lets most sole proprietors deduct up to 20% of qualified business income, limited to 20% of taxable income before it. Above $201,750 of taxable income (single or head of household) or $403,500 (joint) it phases out over $75,000 / $150,000 for a business with no W-2 employees, which is how this calculator treats it. For 2026 there is also a $400 minimum deduction if you have at least $1,000 of qualified business income.

What if I earned money but did not get a 1099?

It is still taxable. The 1099-NEC/1099-MISC reporting threshold only decides whether a client has to send you a form (it rises from $600 to $2,000 for payments made in 2026); you report all net business income on Schedule C.

Does this include state income tax?

No. This is federal only. Some states, such as Texas, Florida, Nevada and Washington, have no tax on personal income, but most states do tax self-employment profit; add your state's tax on top of the total here.

What is not included in this estimate?

Credits (child tax credit, education credits), itemized deductions, capital gains and qualified dividends, net investment income tax, the new tips, overtime, senior and car-loan deductions, alternative minimum tax and state taxes. The QBI deduction is modeled for a sole proprietor with no employees and no business property, and is not adjusted for specified service trades.
