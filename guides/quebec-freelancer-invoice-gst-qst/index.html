Freelancer invoices in Quebec: GST, QST and registration

Published 2026-10-29 · Checked against official sources 2026-10-29 · All guides

Do you have to charge GST and QST, and what must an invoice show? Here is what Revenu Québec says: the small supplier threshold, the information required by invoice amount, and useful links.

Do I have to register for the GST/HST and QST? The small supplier rule

Revenu Québec treats you as a small supplier if your total taxable supplies, including zero-rated supplies, are $30,000 or less in a given calendar quarter and over the four calendar quarters before it. This counts the supplies made worldwide by you and your associates during that period. The total excludes GST/HST and QST, financial services, and sales of capital property (a building or a car, for example). For the GST/HST, the CRA adds that goodwill from the sale of a business is not included either. According to Revenu Québec, one person is generally associated with another when one controls the other: for example two corporations, an individual and a corporation, or a person and a partnership or trust.

A small supplier does not have to register for the GST/HST and QST or collect these taxes.

Exception for taxis and commercial ride-sharing. Revenu Québec says you must register for the GST and QST, whatever your annual taxable sales, if you operate a taxi business. For a driver related to a transportation system operator under an agreement (for example Uber or Lyft) who operates a taxi business, Revenu Québec says you must be registered before your first trip. The CRA says that an independent taxi or commercial ride-sharing driver must register for the GST/HST even as a small supplier. These drivers cannot wait until they reach $30,000.

If you go over the threshold in a single quarter, you stop being a small supplier immediately and must collect tax on the sale that took you over and on every sale after it; the CRA describes the same principle for the GST/HST. Even as a small supplier you can choose to register: you must then collect the taxes, and you can claim input tax credits. Revenu Québec says a business that carries on commercial activities in Quebec and registers for the QST must also register for the GST/HST, and must stay registered for at least one year. For the federal rule outside Quebec, see the small supplier guide.

What goes on the invoice?

Revenu Québec says the GST/HST and QST systems do not require any particular type of invoice or special rule, except for restaurants and taxi operators. However, a registrant must make sure that specific information appears on purchase invoices or other supporting documents, to back up claims for input tax credits (ITCs) or input tax refunds (ITRs).

The required information depends on the total value of the sale (taxes included), in tiers of under $100, $100 to $499.99, and $500 or more. Excerpt from Revenu Québec's table:

Revenu Québec's full table has more rows (for example the tax payable and a description of the goods or services): check it before you build your invoice template.

Calculating the taxes

GST is 5% of the price and QST is 9.975% of the price excluding GST; the rounding rules are in the GST and QST calculation guide. For income tax, see the self-employed calculator and how to file your returns.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to register for the GST/HST and QST as a freelancer?

According to Revenu Québec, a small supplier ($30,000 or less of taxable supplies, including zero-rated ones, made worldwide by you and your associates, in a quarter and over the previous four) does not have to register; above the threshold, you must collect the taxes. Exception: a taxi business operator must register whatever their taxable sales, and a driver related to a transportation system operator under an agreement (such as Uber or Lyft) before the first trip.

Is there a mandatory invoice template?

Revenu Québec says no particular type of invoice is required, except for restaurants and taxi operators.

Can I register before reaching $30,000?

Yes. Revenu Québec says a small supplier can choose to register and must then collect the taxes. It also says a business carrying on commercial activities in Quebec that registers for the QST must register for the GST/HST too, and stay registered for at least one year.
