How the TFSA limit is set, and how much room you have in total

Published 2026-10-25 · Checked against official sources 2026-10-25 · All guides

The TFSA annual limit is not a political announcement each year. It is $5,000 indexed to inflation and rounded to the nearest $500, which is why it moves in $500 steps. If you have been eligible since 2009, your total room through 2026 is $109,000.

The rule behind the number

Under the Income Tax Act (s.207.01), the TFSA dollar limit is $5,000 for 2009, indexed each year after 2009 using the same inflation factor as the tax brackets (s.117.1) and rounded to the nearest $500. The Act sets $10,000 for 2015 as a one-off. That explains the zig-zag in the table below: the limit only changes when accumulated inflation crosses a rounding step.

Total room by when you became eligible

You start accruing room in the calendar year you turn 18, as long as you are a resident of Canada with a SIN. If you became eligible later than 2009, your lifetime total is the sum from your first year. Nothing contributed:

These are totals before any contributions or withdrawals. Your actual room is that total, minus everything you have contributed, plus withdrawals from previous years.

What withdrawals do to room

A TFSA withdrawal does not create room right away. It is added back on January 1 of the next calendar year. That timing is the source of most accidental over-contributions (see the penalty guide). Withdrawing $5,000 in March 2026 means $5,000 of extra room from January 1, 2027, not before.

What about next year's limit?

Not yet announced as of October 25, 2026. The CRA publishes the next year's limit in the fall. Numbers floating around before then are guesses: we will update this page and the calculator on the day the CRA confirms it.

Checking your own number

Your CRA account shows your room as of the CRA's last annual update, which happens in spring. It does not include this year's deposits and withdrawals. The safe method is the CRA figure plus your own records since: use the calculator to rebuild room from your first eligible year.

Frequently asked questions

How is the TFSA limit decided?

By formula: $5,000 for 2009, indexed to inflation each year and rounded to the nearest $500, with $10,000 for 2015 set in the Act.

What is the total TFSA room if I have been eligible since 2009?

$109,000 through 2026 (the sum of the annual limits), before subtracting anything you contributed.

When do TFSA withdrawals get added back to my room?

On January 1 of the following calendar year, not immediately.

Is the 2027 TFSA limit announced?

Not as of October 25, 2026. The CRA announces it in the fall; we will update when it is official.
