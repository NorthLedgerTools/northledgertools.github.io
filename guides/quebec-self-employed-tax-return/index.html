Self-employed in Quebec: how to report your business income

Published 2026-10-29 · Checked against official sources 2026-10-29 · All guides

If you are self-employed in Quebec, you file two returns, one with Revenu Québec and one with the CRA, each with a statement of your business income and expenses. Here are the steps, forms and dates that matter, based on Revenu Québec and the CRA.

  2. The forms
  3. The deadlines
  4. Estimating what you will owe

The steps

  1. Keep your records. Revenu Québec's checklist asks you to record your income and expenses regularly and to keep every document needed to support them.
  2. Work out your net business income. Revenu Québec suggests calculating this amount, then using the tax tables to estimate the tax and contributions you will owe each year.
  3. Plan for instalments. Revenu Québec suggests planning the instalments you will have to pay, if any.
  4. File your returns. Your income tax return, your sales tax returns if you are registered for the GST and QST, and any other returns that apply.

The forms

In Quebec, form TP-80-V (Business or Professional Income and Expenses) is for people who carry on a business, which includes practising a profession and self-employed commission work. Federally, form T2125 is used to report business or professional income and expenses.

The deadlines

Revenu Québec says the filing deadline is April 30, but you have until June 15 if you or your spouse carried on a business during the tax year. However, any balance owing must be paid by April 30; after that date, Revenu Québec charges interest on the unpaid balance. When a deadline falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it moves to the next business day.

The CRA shows the same federal dates (2026 dates shown by the CRA, for the 2025 return): June 15 to file if you or your spouse or common-law partner are self-employed, and April 30 to pay.

Key point Even if you cannot pay your whole balance, file by the deadline to avoid a late-filing penalty (Revenu Québec).

Frequently asked questions

What is the filing deadline for self-employed people in Quebec?

Revenu Québec gives June 15 if you or your spouse carried on a business; any balance owing is still due by April 30.

Which form do I fill in for Quebec?

Form TP-80-V, Business or Professional Income and Expenses, if you carry on a business; federally, form T2125.

What if I cannot pay my whole balance?

According to Revenu Québec, file your return by the filing deadline anyway to avoid the late-filing penalty.
