What we compare

Revenu Québec publishes two 2026 tables for self-employed workers: provincial and federal income tax at 22 levels of taxable income, and QPP, QPIP and Health Services Fund contributions at 22 levels of net business income. That is 44 tax figures (22 rows, provincial and federal) and 66 contribution figures (22 rows, three contributions). Our test runs the same calculation engine that powers the Quebec calculators on this site and compares each result with the published value, rounded to the dollar.

Why it matters

Quebec self-employed tax involves federal tax with the 16.5% abatement, QPP with an employee and an employer share, QPIP, and the Health Services Fund. Mistakes in any one of these (for instance treating QPP as CPP) show up as dollars. Testing against the official table catches them.

What the test does not prove

  • Revenu Québec says its tables are an overview and not an official calculation. They describe a single filer with no other credits; real returns vary.
  • The tables cover Quebec. For Ontario, BC and Alberta we use the published federal and provincial brackets, rates and credits, but we are not aware of an equivalent official table of results to test against, so treat those estimates with a little more caution.
  • Our calculators estimate for one person with no family credits. They are for planning, not filing.

Our process for the rest of the content

  1. Every number is taken from an official page (CRA, Revenu Québec, Retraite Québec, IRS, the Income Tax Act) and the source is cited on the page.
  2. Guides are drafted with the help of AI tools, then each figure is checked against its source.
  3. When a limit changes (January for most), we update the calculators and guides together and show the date checked.
  4. If we cannot confirm a number from an official source, we say so rather than guess. As of October 25, 2026, that applies to the 2027 TFSA limit.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the Quebec calculators?

In our latest test, 110 of 110 figures from Revenu Québec's 2026 self-employed tables matched to the dollar.

Do you test Ontario, BC and Alberta too?

Those are checked against CRA brackets and worked examples; there is no official results table equivalent to Revenu Québec's to test against.

Can I re-run the test?

Yes. The repository includes validation/rq_check.js and the official figures; run it with Node.
