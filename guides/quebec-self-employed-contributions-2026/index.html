Quebec self-employed contributions in 2026: QPP, QPIP and HSF

Published 2026-10-29 · Checked against official sources 2026-10-29 · All guides

An employee pays only part of these contributions; a self-employed person pays all of it. In 2026, at $60,000 of net business income, Revenu Québec's table shows $7,119 of QPP, $458 of QPIP and $150 to the Health Services Fund: $7,727 in total, before income tax.

Self-employed people pay both shares of the QPP, the employee's and the employer's, which is why the rate is 12.6% rather than half that. Revenu Québec notes that contributions are calculated on net business income, while income tax is calculated on taxable income (after, for example, the RRSP deduction).

Three things stand out: the QPP tops out at $9,791 around $85,000 of income, QPIP at $787 above $103,000, and the HSF rises to $1,000. Above $85,000 the total barely moves, which is why the share of income you need to set aside falls as income rises.

Example: $60,000 of net income

QPP: 12.6% × ($60,000 − $3,500) = $7,119. QPIP: 0.764% × $60,000 = $458.40, rounded to $458. HSF: $150. Total: $7,727, about 12.9% of net income, before income tax. These amounts match Revenu Québec's table.

What is deductible

Part of your QPP and QPIP contributions can be deducted from income, and the rest gives a non-refundable tax credit. The exact rules are in Revenu Québec's and the CRA's forms; our calculator applies them to estimate your tax and is tested against the official tables (see how we check our numbers).

Frequently asked questions

What is the QPP rate for self-employed people in 2026?

12.6% of net business income between $3,500 and $74,600, then 8% between $74,600 and $85,000. Maximum: $9,791.

How much QPIP does a self-employed person pay?

0.764% of income up to $103,000, so at most $786.92 in 2026.

Why do I pay more than an employee?

Because a self-employed person pays both the employee's share and the employer's share.

Are contributions calculated on taxable income?

No. They are calculated on net business income; income tax is calculated on taxable income.
