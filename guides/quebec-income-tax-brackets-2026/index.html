Quebec income tax in 2026: brackets and rates

Published 2026-10-29 · Checked against official sources 2026-10-29 · All guides

Quebec uses progressive rates: each rate applies only to the slice of taxable income that falls inside its bracket. Here are Revenu Québec's brackets for 2026 and how to estimate provincial tax before credits.

The 2026 brackets

Revenu Québec publishes these income tax rates for the 2026 taxation year, based on taxable income:

Taxable income (2026)Rate
$54,345 or less14%
More than $54,345, up to $108,68019%
More than $108,680, up to $132,24524%
More than $132,24525.75%

Compared with 2025

The rates are the same in 2025, but the bracket thresholds are lower:

Taxable income (2025)Rate
$53,255 or less14%
More than $53,255, up to $106,49519%
More than $106,495, up to $129,59024%
More than $129,59025.75%

How the bracket calculation works

Apply each rate to the part of taxable income that sits in its bracket, then add the results. Example for $70,000 of taxable income in 2026:

Slice of incomeRateTax

Frequently asked questions

What are Quebec's income tax rates for 2026?

14%, 19%, 24% and 25.75%, depending on the taxable income bracket (see Revenu Québec's table).

Do I pay 25.75% on all my income once I earn over $132,245?

No. 25.75% applies only to the part of taxable income above $132,245; the rest is taxed at the lower brackets' rates.

Does this calculation give my exact tax?

No. It is an estimate before credits. Your tax depends on your full return.
